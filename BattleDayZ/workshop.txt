Battle royale rounds on Nasdara, Chernarus and Livonia, solo, last one standing wins. Everyone starts each round equal, jumps from a plane, loots fast and races the storm into a shrinking circle.

[h2]A round[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]The lobby:[/b] everyone waits in a copy of a real place from the map, floating a kilometre up in the sky: Zub castle, Chernogorsk square or Prison Island on Chernarus, Qalat-e-Lut, the airport junkyard or Kirov dam on Nasdara, Branzow castle, Brena square or Lukow airfield on Livonia, a different one each round. You can't be hurt there, walking off the edge just puts you back, and everyone is reset to the same plain kit at full health, with gloves, a belt and a pistol holster. A countdown at the top of the screen shows when the next round starts; press [b]F[/b] when you're ready, and once everyone waiting is, it starts straight away.
[*] [b]The plane:[/b] it crosses the whole map edge to edge, its route drawn on the map. You watch from behind it and can orbit it with the mouse. Press [b]F[/b] to jump.
[*] [b]The jump:[/b] a long free fall lying flat, steering with the movement keys. Press [b]F[/b] again to open the parachute, or it opens by itself 100 m up. Then glide down under a steerable canopy that everyone can see, and land on the ground or on a roof. No damage from the jump.
[*] [b]Loot:[/b] fourteen guns, two per calibre (9 mm, .45, .357, .308, 12 gauge, 7.62x39, 5.56x45), loaded and with spare ammo next to them; reload fills a magazine straight from your ammo boxes and loads it. Three tiers of helmets, vests and backpacks. Sights (Mini Sights, Baraka, ACOG, Combat Sights, hunting scope, with batteries in) and suppressors that fit NATO and Soviet guns alike, extended mags, frag grenades, Molotovs, bandages, morphine and jerry cans. Supply drops of top-tier gear come down inside the next circle.
[*] [b]Vehicles:[/b] 100 of them, enduro motorbikes, Ada 4x4s, M1025s and Land Rovers, with every part fitted and a little fuel. They're spread evenly over the whole map: at vanilla car spots, parked by whatever is built nearby, or out in the open, with nothing guaranteed anywhere. Chernarus and Livonia also get boats, on the water at vanilla boat spots.
[*] [b]The circle:[/b] after 5 minutes the first circle is announced in white, and a red circle closes in on it from around the whole map. Then it repeats: the next circle is announced in white, the one you're in turns yellow, and after a countdown the yellow turns red and closes on the white. It gets faster each circle until it closes to nothing.
[*] [b]The storm:[/b] outside the circle you're in a gas zone, with the gas, the tint and the sound. A glowing red wall with gas behind it shows where the edge is, and glides in as it closes. Every few seconds the storm hits you: you flinch, grunt and sometimes start bleeding, and it hurts more each circle. You can still bandage in it.
[*] [b]Knocked out?[/b] Watch the rest of the round: the camera follows a player still in it, [b]F[/b] moves on to the next.
[*] [b]The end:[/b] the last player alive wins and everyone is told. Then everything the round spawned is cleared away and the next round starts.
[/list]

[h2]Also[/h2]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
Players and the server both need: Community Framework, Dabs Framework, DayZ-Expansion-Licensed, DayZ-Expansion-Core, DayZ-Expansion-Navigation, DayZ-Expansion-Missions, DayZ-Expansion-Vehicles, Flying Without Crash To Desktop, Molotov Cocktail, Better Explosions and Better Explosions - Expansion Vehicles.

A Nasdara server also needs [b]BattleDayZ Nasdara[/b], and a Livonia server [b]BattleDayZ Livonia[/b] (server only, players don't need them), which hold those maps' own places for the lobby. Other maps can get the same kind of small add-on.

The server also needs the battle royale mission for its map (Nasdara, Chernarus or Livonia), which holds the settings, the loot spots, and the Central Economy switched off. Solo only for now (no teams). Played and tested in about a dozen three-player rounds.
